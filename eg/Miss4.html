Talking about work and describing jobs

Talking about Work

Complete the dialogue below with the correct questions.

Possible questions:

What’s your job title?

What are your responsibilities?

What qualifications do you need?

What skills do you need?

What do you like?

A: What’s your job title?

B: I work as a bank teller.

A: What are your responsibilities?

B: I’m responsible for serving customers in a bank. For example, answering questions and exchanging money.

A: What qualifications do you need?

B: For this job, you only need a high school diploma. You don’t need a university degree.

A: What skills do you need?

B: You need to be good at counting money. Also, you must be friendly because you work with customers.

A: What do you like?

B: My job is sometimes a little boring, but it’s secure.
